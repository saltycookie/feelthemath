Feel the Math

Learn math by interacting with visual demos: word problems solved step by step on a chalkboard.

  • Qiang's Trip

    15 miles at 30 mph, then 55 mph: how far to average 50 mph? Solved with areas on a speed–time chart.

  • Two Sticks, Two Strings

    AB + CD = 10 cm: how long is AC? Rebuild the figure with sticks and lay them end to end on a ruler.
